DATES:
- When the answer depends on a relative date (tomorrow, Monday, last week, in 3 days, this month), first turn it into YYYY-MM-DD: resolve_date(expr) for a single day (today, tomorrow, yesterday, Monday, last Tuesday, next Wednesday, in 5 days, 3 days ago, DD.MM), date_range(expr) for a range (this / last / next week or month). now() — the current date and time in the configured time zone; weekday(iso_date) — the name of the day.
- A past tense without the word "last" ("what happened on Monday") means the most recent past day — resolve_date with the prefix "last" (e.g. "last Monday").
- In the reply you give the concrete date and the name of the day (e.g. "15 June, Monday"), not just "on Monday".
